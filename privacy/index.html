Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

The short version

Hush collects nothing about you. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. The app cannot see who calls you, even if it wanted to.

That is not a promise about how we behave. It is a description of how the app is built, and the rest of this page explains why it could not be otherwise.

What we collect

Nothing.

ContactsNot accessed. Hush never asks.
Call historyNot accessed.
Who is calling youNot visible to Hush at all. See below.
Your phone numberNot collected.
LocationNot accessed.
MicrophoneNot accessed.
MessagesNot accessed.
Advertising identifierNot accessed.
Name, email, accountNever asked for. There is no sign up.
Usage or analyticsNot collected. Hush contains no analytics software.

Hush does not display advertisements and does not sell, share, or transfer data to anyone, because it does not have any.

Why Hush cannot see your calls

Hush uses Apple's Call Directory system. The app gives iOS a list of phone numbers in advance. When a call arrives, iOS checks that list itself. Hush is not running, is not consulted, and is never told that a call happened.

This is why Hush cannot show you a count of blocked calls. Some people expect that feature. It cannot exist without the app seeing your calls, so it does not exist here.

What is stored on your iPhone

One setting: whether you have finished the introduction. It never leaves your device.

The list of blocked numbers is not stored anywhere. It is calculated on your iPhone when protection is switched on.

Internet and servers

Hush has no servers. There is no backend to hold your data, because there is no company system for your data to reach.

The app itself never connects to the internet. Blocking works with no signal, in aeroplane mode, and on a network you have never used before.

The only network activity associated with Hush is Apple's own, when you buy or restore a subscription. That is handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your payment details, and Apple's privacy policy governs it.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Hush receives only whether an active subscription exists on your device. It does not receive your name, your email address, your payment method, or any Apple ID information.

We cannot identify you, and we cannot link a subscription to a person.

Children

Hush does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Any change that reduces your privacy would require an app update, which you would have to choose to install.
