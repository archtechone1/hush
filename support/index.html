Setting up

Hush says it is switched off

Open Settings, then Apps, then Phone, then Call Blocking and Identification, and turn on Hush. This is the only setup Hush needs. It is a switch, not a permission: it grants Hush no access to anything.

Setting up takes a while the first time

That is expected. Hush writes its list of blocked numbers into iOS, which takes about fifteen seconds. It happens again only if you turn the switch off and on, reinstall Hush, or resubscribe after a break.

Blocking

A 140-series call still got through

Hush covers the parts of the 140 series that telemarketers actually use, based on the regulator's published allocation of that series. It does not claim to block every possible number. Hush deliberately has no way to report a call to us, and we do not collect numbers from you. If you want to report an unwanted call, the official route is the TRAI DND app or a message to 1909, which reaches your operator and can lead to action against the caller.

A call I wanted was blocked

This should not happen. The 140 series is reserved for commercial calls, so no personal caller uses it. If it does happen, please tell us at once, because a blocked real caller is the most serious problem this app can have.

Does Hush label calls or report numbers?

No. Hush has no report button and no shared list. It never marks a call as spam for anyone, and nothing you do in Hush changes what any other phone sees. Blocking happens on your iPhone, by iOS, because you switched it on, and it affects only your phone.

What about 1600 and 1601 numbers?

Hush never touches them. Those series are reserved for banks, government and service calls, and they always ring.

Is there an official way to stop promotional calls?

Yes. India's regulator lets you register your preference with your operator by dialling 1909 or using the TRAI DND app. Registered telemarketers must then stop calling you. Hush works alongside that: it stops the 140 series calls on your own phone, whether or not the caller respects your registration.

Can I see which calls were blocked?

No, and this is deliberate. Hush never sees who calls you, which is what lets us promise it collects nothing. A blocked calls list would require watching your calls. You can see blocked calls in your iPhone's own Recents list, marked by iOS.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then Hush, then Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before your period ends to avoid the next charge.

I was charged after my trial

Free trials convert automatically unless cancelled, which the app states before purchase. For refunds, use Apple's process at reportaproblem.apple.com, since Apple handles all payments and we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

I changed phones and lost my subscription

Open Settings inside Hush and tap Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple Account.

Blocking stopped after my subscription ended

That is expected. Resubscribe from the app to turn it back on.

Privacy

What do you collect?

Nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

How can that be true?

Your iPhone does the blocking. Hush gives iOS a list in advance and is never told when a call arrives.
