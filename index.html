Hush

Block 140-series spam calls. Built in India.

A firewall for your phone calls

Hush blocks 140-series telemarketing calls silently, before your phone rings. No ringtone, no vibration, no banner.

Privacy is the product

Hush never sees who calls you. That is not a limitation, it is the design.

Your iPhone does the blocking. Hush hands iOS a list in advance, and iOS checks it when a call arrives. The app is not running and is never told a call happened.

No contacts. No account. No tracking. No ads. No servers.
