What Hush does and does not promise

Hush blocks calls from the parts of India's 140 series that telemarketers use. It does not claim to block every possible number, and unwanted calls from outside that series are not affected. Hush never blocks, labels or touches calls from the 1600 or 1601 series, which are reserved for banks, government and service calls.

Blocking is performed by iOS, not by Hush, and requires you to keep Hush switched on under Call Blocking and Identification.
